# Research rules

These add to `ig-viral`, which holds the method.

## When

Competitor research needs the user present. It runs at setup and in check-ins, never in a weekly run. If `swipe.md` is over 30 days old, suggest a check-in in the weekly summary.

## How much

- 6 to 12 accounts, about 12 reels each, at human speed, approved by the user.
- At least half should be within 10x of the user's follower count.
- At most about 10 page loads between pauses.

## Stop signals

Stop reading Instagram immediately on "Try again later", "Please wait a few minutes", a 429 or an unexpected login page. Don't retry in that run. Tell the user and carry on with what you have.

If 3 page loads in a row fail, the browser is probably stalled or hidden. Stop and treat the data as missing.

## What gets recorded

- Rank by outlier multiple: views divided by that account's own median.
- Record the formula, never a copy of the script or video. Attribute each row.
- State the sample size and confidence. Under 40 reels is weak.
- Skip accounts whose content the brand wouldn't want to be associated with.
